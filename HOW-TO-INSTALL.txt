MONEY DIARY - install it as an app on your phone
================================================

Your entries are stored only on your phone. Nothing is sent to any server.
GitHub only hosts the app's code, the same way the Play Store does.

OPTION A - Install as an app (recommended, takes 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. On GitHub, create a new repository, for example "money-diary".
2. Upload all the files from this folder (index.html, sw.js,
   manifest.webmanifest and the 4 icon .png files) to the repository.
3. In the repository, open Settings > Pages.
   Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder: / (root). Save.
4. After a minute, your app is at:
   https://<your-github-username>.github.io/money-diary/
5. Open that link on your phone in Chrome.
   Menu (3 dots) > "Add to Home screen" > Install.
   On iPhone: open it in Safari > Share > "Add to Home Screen".
6. Open Money Diary from the icon. It opens full screen and works with
   no internet.

Want a real .apk file? Go to https://www.pwabuilder.com, paste the link
from step 4, choose Android and download the package. You can install
that .apk on your phone.

OPTION B - No setup
-------------------
Copy Money-Diary.html to your phone and open it in Chrome. Entries are
saved in that browser. Opening the file this way doesn't work in every
phone browser, so Option A is more reliable.

GOOGLE DRIVE SYNC (optional, one-time setup, about 10 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Your data is saved to a hidden app folder in YOUR Google Drive. Only
this app can read it. It doesn't show up among your normal Drive files
and is never shared with anyone. It works in the app installed from
your GitHub link (Option A), not in the single Money-Diary.html file.

1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Google
   account. Create a new project, for example "Money Diary".
2. Go to APIs & Services > Library, search "Google Drive API" and click
   Enable.
3. Go to APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen (also called "Google
   Auth Platform"). Choose External. Fill in the app name and your
   email. Under Audience / Test users, add your own Gmail address.
4. Go to APIs & Services > Credentials > Create credentials >
   OAuth client ID. Application type: Web application.
   Under "Authorized JavaScript origins" add:
       https://<your-github-username>.github.io
   Click Create and copy the Client ID
   (it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com).
5. In Money Diary, open Settings > Google Drive sync and tap
   "Connect Google Drive". The Client ID is already built into the app.
   Choose your Google account.
   Google may warn "Google hasn't verified this app". That's expected
   for an app only you use: tap Continue.
6. Do the same on your other phone or computer (same Client ID) and
   both stay in sync.

How syncing works:
- Every entry is saved on the phone first, so the app works offline.
- It syncs to Drive when the app opens, when you come back online,
  a few seconds after each change, and every 5 minutes.
- If you edit on two devices, both sets of changes are kept. If the
  same entry was edited on both, the newer edit wins. Deleted entries
  are deleted everywhere.
- Google sign-in lasts about an hour. After a longer break the top pill
  shows "Tap to sync". Tap it once and sign-in continues.
- Your PIN is not synced. Set it separately on each device.

KEEP YOUR DATA SAFE
-------------------
- Settings > Export to Excel saves everything to one .xlsx file.
  Do this every week or two. The app reminds you after 14 days.
- Settings > Import from Excel brings everything back on a new phone,
  or after you clear the browser's data.
- You can add rows in Excel too. Leave the ID blank for new rows.
  Type must be one of: I gave, I got, Split bill, My expense, Family,
  Income. Date as YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY.
- If you forget your PIN, "Forgot PIN?" erases the data on this phone
  only. If Drive sync is on, nothing is lost: after you sign in to
  Google, everything comes back from your Drive. Without Drive sync,
  import your latest Excel backup.
- Clearing the browser's data or changing phones: with Drive sync on,
  connect Drive again and all your entries come back. Without it, use
  your Excel backup.
