MONEY DIARY - install it as an app on your phone
================================================

1. PUT IT ONLINE (GitHub Pages)
   - Upload every file in this folder to github.com/Birlaravi/money-diary
     (Add file > Upload files > Commit changes). Replace the old files.
   - Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root) > Save.
   - After a minute or two the app is at:
       https://birlaravi.github.io/money-diary/

2. INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE
   - Open that link in Chrome > menu (3 dots) > Add to Home screen > Install.
   - iPhone: open it in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.
   - It opens full screen and works with no internet.

3. GOOGLE DRIVE SYNC
   - In the app: Menu > Backup & restore > Connect Google Drive.
   - Sign in with your Google account. If Google says "Google hasn't
     verified this app", tap Continue.
   - From then on it syncs by itself whenever you are online.

   Who can sign in:
   - Each person signs in with their own Google account and only ever
     sees their own data. Nobody can see yours.
   - While the Google project is in "Testing" mode, only the Gmail
     addresses listed under Google Cloud Console > Google Auth Platform >
     Audience > Test users can connect Drive. Add anyone else who will
     use the app there (up to 100 people).
   - The app itself works for everyone without signing in. Their data
     then stays only on their own phone.

4. KEEP YOUR DATA SAFE
   - Menu > Backup & restore > Export to Excel saves everything
     (entries, people, repeating entries, budgets) in one .xlsx file.
   - Import from Excel brings it all back on any phone.
   - In Excel you can add rows too. Leave the ID blank for new rows.
     Type must be one of: I gave, I got, Split bill, My expense, Family,
     Income. Date as YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY.
   - Forgot your PIN? "Forgot PIN?" erases the data on that phone. With
     Drive connected, everything comes back after you sign in to Google.
     Without Drive, import your latest Excel file.

Want a real .apk? Paste the app link into https://www.pwabuilder.com,
choose Android and download the package.
